# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- **Added.** Cast sessions: while the TV plays the server polls its position every 10 s and saves watch progress; "Stop on TV" / "Watch here instead" now resume from the TV's actual position, not where the laptop was; reloading the player page reconnects to the running TV session automatically.
- **Fixed.** After casting to a TV the video no longer starts again on the computer as well: the player stays paused, shows "Playing on <TV>", and offers Watch here instead (stops the TV and carries on here) or Stop on TV.
- **Added.** Cast to TVs with Chromecast built-in (Google TV, Android TV such as Sony Bravia, Chromecast sticks) straight from the player menu, in any browser: the server talks to the TV directly and it plays in the TV's own player. TVs are found by asking each address on the home network, because a firewall on the server usually drops the replies to the multicast search; nothing to switch on in the TV.
- **Changed.** Casting to a TV: the search for TVs and boxes that can play a video sent to them (DLNA) now runs on the host's network (the netinfo service), because the dashboard's container never saw them; iPhone and iPad offer AirPlay from the player. A TV's own player decodes HEVC in hardware, so casting is the smooth way to watch on a TV whose browser has no acceleration.
- **Changed.** Without graphics acceleration in the browser: video starts at 720p (converted on the server, much lighter to decode and draw; Auto is still one tap away), GPU-only visualizer scenes are hidden and fall back to Bars, and a one-time tip says where to switch acceleration on.
- **Added.** Hardware video conversion on an Intel or AMD graphics chip (VA-API), with docker-compose.gpu.yml. The app checks at start-up that the GPU really encodes and otherwise uses the CPU.
- **Fixed.** Video stuttered on a TV and in Chromium browsers. A converted stream now stays near the source's own bitrate (an efficient 1 Mbit/s HEVC episode was being sent at about 10 Mbit/s over Wi-Fi), and a browser that would decode HEVC in software (no graphics acceleration) gets a converted H.264 stream instead.
- **Added.** Light mode for TVs and browsers without graphics acceleration (detected, or by a short frame-rate check): no frosted-glass blur or large shadows, and the background visualizers (bottom bar, Music page) pause; everything still works. TV browsers also get a larger interface.
- **Fixed.** In the full-screen visualizer a tap now brings the controls back (it needed a drag). English subtitles no longer switch on by themselves when an English episode opens. Notices in the player sit above its controls.
- **Changed.** English by default for films and episodes in another language (a Bollywood film, say): when the file also carries an English dub, playback starts with the English audio; when it does not, English subtitles come on. English-language titles are left alone. Turning subtitles off in the player is remembered for the next film.
- **Changed.** Subtitle sources: subf2m is now set up (it needs a browser identity), Podnapisi is added, and tvsubtitles is switched off (its search page no longer exists). New installs get the same setup.
- **Fixed.** Subtitles were never found: the subtitle service had no DNS servers, so it could not reach a single subtitle site, yet answered "done" to every search, and the app said "Subtitles added". It now has public resolvers like the other services, its match score is 60 instead of 90 (90 rejected nearly every subtitle for web releases), and the app checks that a subtitle file really exists before saying so.
- **Changed.** Pulled or uploaded subtitles switch on in the player straight away (no reload), with a small notice "English subtitles on" and Undo.
- **Added.** Notices in the corner when something finishes in the background: a request that is ready ("… is ready · Watch") or could not be found ("See why"). Any page can show one.
- **Changed.** Features that need one more service (YouTube downloads, Videos) show the one command with a Copy button and keep checking, so the page carries on by itself once it is on. A conversion session that ended restarts on its own. Renaming and deleting a photo album use the app's own dialogs instead of the browser's pop-ups. Messages that said "reload the page" now say what happened.
- **Fixed.** In full screen the mouse pointer vanished over the picture and showed only over the controls: an old rule hid the pointer on the whole watch page and only restored it while the page was hovered, which full screen does not count. The player now sets its own pointer, hidden only after a few still seconds while playing.
- **Added.** TV specials (Season 0) come from video sites when the torrent sites do not have them: YouTube, Dailymotion and the Internet Archive are searched, the video whose title (and length, when known) fits best is saved into the show's Season 00 folder as "Show - S00E05 - Title" and the TV manager imports it. "Find missing specials online" on a show's Specials tab, and "Search for this episode" on a special, both do this. For How I Met Your Mother 6 of 13 specials were found and imported.
- **Changed.** Seasons that are still mostly missing get a whole-season search (packs), on "Search for missing" and in the 12-hourly backlog search: the TV manager only tries season packs by itself when every episode of a season is missing, so a season that started one episode at a time stayed that way.
- **Fixed.** "Search for this episode" now shows up in Requests (on the show's request, or a new one), so the search can be followed.
- **Changed.** The video player was redone: the film fills the window (the site menus step aside), a top bar with back, the title and the episode; large centre controls when paused; a full-width seek bar with elapsed and remaining time; icon buttons, with quality, speed and audio in one settings menu. The pointer hides only after a few still seconds while playing, and comes back on any movement, in full screen too.
- **Added.** Frame rate for the visualizers (Now Playing > Visuals): 24 like film, 30 like video, or 60 smooth (the default). Frames keep to an even schedule, so 24 on a 60 Hz screen averages exactly 24, and motion follows real time, so a lower rate never slows the picture down.
- **Fixed.** The scene previews in the full-screen picker were blank: the rule that pauses everything behind the full-screen view paused them too.
- **Changed.** Mandelbrot is now an endless, seamless zoom: near c = i the set repeats itself after a 4·√2 zoom and a 45° turn, so the view loops through that step forever with no cut, coloured by distance to the set (smooth, no grain) with crisp anti-aliased filaments. Visualizers always draw at the display's own resolution (up to 4K); the quality setting and the resolution scaler are gone. Full views draw GPU scenes straight into their own canvas (no copy per frame). Faster without lowering quality: per-frame values (spark positions and colours) are worked out once on the CPU instead of per pixel, the tree draws one stroke per branch level instead of thousands, Alchemy is crisp particles with stamped halos, Kaleidoscope uses one noise pass, Julia stops early inside the set. The old CPU versions of GPU scenes were removed.

Channels show the programme on now with its progress, the player shows now and next, and the Guide view lists the next hours (about half of India's free channels are covered).
- **Changed.** Visualizer quality setting in Now Playing > Visuals: Battery saver (720p, 30 frames a second), Balanced (up to 1440p, the default) or Ultra (native up to 4K). Only one visualizer draws at a time (the full-screen view pauses the rest), and any scrolled out of view stops drawing.
- **Changed.** Mandelbrot zooms up to a million times and stays sharp (deeper zooms broke up). The tunnel never slows or stops on the bass any more: kicks flare the rings and send a shockwave instead. Alchemy is drawn on the graphics card and is crisp at any size.

- **Fixed.** Seeking a converted video still failed with "This browser could not decode the video" after the previous fix to this same error, specifically on Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Brave, Edge...). Root cause this time was different: newer Chromium builds now also answer truthy for `canPlayType('video/vnd.apple.mpegurl')` (an experimental native HLS player of their own), which this app's browser check took as "this is Safari, use HLS" - the one case it was actually written for. Confirmed live with a real headless Chromium reproduction (login, navigate, seek, read the actual `MediaError`): Chromium's own HLS demuxer rejected this server's output with `DEMUXER_ERROR_COULD_NOT_PARSE` on every seek, even though the exact same content played perfectly through the plain progressive stream Chromium already used before gaining this HLS capability. Narrowed the check to actual Safari (true WebKit, not a Chromium browser merely built on it), so Chromium stays on the progressive path that was already proven to work, and Safari keeps the HLS it genuinely needs. Verified live across 6 seek positions spanning a full movie: no decode errors, no `MediaError` of any kind.

Confirmed live: probed the actual transcoded output for a seek to 25:00 in a real movie - it now starts at timestamp ~0 instead of ~1500s, and decodes cleanly.

Downloads open on what matters (in progress, or what needs attention), show total speed, and can pause or resume everything at once.
- **Changed.** Settings is rebuilt: a guided overview ("What do you want to do?" with live status and suggested next steps), a sidebar of eleven sections, a search box that finds a setting by what you would type, clearer names (Services, Search sources, People and requests, Network and devices, Health and repair), and an administrator-only trail of who changed what (never any secret). Old links keep working.
- **Added.** Photos: a timeline by month, folders, search, your own albums and favorites, and a full-screen viewer with swipe or arrow keys, zoom, slideshow with speed, details, and download of the original. The photo folder stays read only.
- **Added.** Live TV: favorites and recently watched per person, categories, a programme guide from XMLTV (now and next on every row, and a timeline grid), channel numbers with keyboard and digit zapping, a small picture-in-picture window, several sources, a check that hides channels that do not answer, and a sticky player on phones.
- **Changed.** Books (search the whole shelf, format filters, sort, favorites), Wiki (a two-step start, reader with a toolbar), Apps (a tile per app with status and one Open button), Statistics (what needs a look, a stacked storage bar), Themes and Account (profile card, quick links, devices) use the same design.
- **Fixed.** Downloads that nobody shares ("stalled with no connections") or that finish but cannot be matched to their title ("Couldn't find similar album") sat in the queue for ever, because Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr never give up on them. A new download doctor checks the queue every five minutes and, after a wait (about 45 minutes for a dead release, 20 for unmatched files), rejects the release so it is never picked again and searches for another. It never acts while the downloader or VPN is down, leaves problems a new release cannot fix (full disk, permissions) to a person, replaces a title at most three times a day, and tells administrators in the bell. The Downloads page now explains each stuck download in plain words, says when it will be replaced, and has a **Fix stuck now** button. It can be turned off under Settings.
- **Changed.** Scrollbars are slim and quiet everywhere; sideways rows (cast, categories, formats) get an arrow at each end on a computer, so Live TV categories can be reached without a touchpad. The three-dots buttons are round, matched in size, and their menu is a sheet above the tab bar on phones. Buttons in Settings keep the size of their words. "TV" is now "TV shows" and "Live" is "Live TV".
- **Added.** The theme creator shows a live copy of the app that updates as you change anything, and can paint the whole app with the theme while you edit (yours comes back when you leave).
- **Fixed.** The three-dots menu was cut off inside a hero or card, and its dots sat off-centre. The menu is now drawn above the page, opens upward when there is no room below, and the dots are centred in a round button everywhere.
- **Changed.** Live TV's player pane is larger (and has a Bigger player button); the theme creator's preview stays in view while you scroll the settings; the Restart buttons' "helper is off" message now says plainly what it is and how to turn it on, and docs/services-helper.md explains the Podman sleep case.
- **Added.** A tab that stays open across an update now says "A new version of virtuallyView is ready" with a Reload button, instead of quietly running the old page.
- **Changed.** Live TV shows a LIVE badge when the picture is at the live edge, "N s behind · Go live" when it is not, and "Recording" when a stream is not live at all. Sport channels say that free public sport channels usually replay classic events, and that a race weekend happening right now is normally on a paid broadcaster.
- **Fixed.** A movie, show or artist that was still being requested or downloaded could not be removed: the page sometimes carried the request's id ("request-110") instead of the library id, so the media manager refused the delete. The library list no longer shows a second placeholder row for a title that is already in the media manager (it now matches by the linked id, and ignores "The" in titles), and removing a title now handles either id, stops and removes its running downloads first (so they do not keep going with nothing to import into), and drops the requests that pointed at it.
- **Fixed.** Removing a show that was downloading left its torrents running in qBittorrent: Sonarr's own queue view lists only a page of rows, so most were missed. Removing a title now also finds its torrents in the download client by the title they belong to, and stops them (deleting their partial files when "delete files" is chosen).
- **Added.** `scripts/title-backup.mjs` backs up and restores one TV show: its files, checksums, how Sonarr tracks it, monitored episodes and watch progress, and proves the restore by checking every file. See docs/administration.md.
- **Added.** Use folders on your computer for the library: set `MOVIES_DIR`, `TV_DIR` and `MUSIC_DIR` in `.env` and the apps use them (default unchanged: Docker-managed storage). Before this there was no folder to put existing files in. See docs/existing-media.md; Settings, Library and quality now explains it too.
