# Rights and acceptable use

virtuallyView is software for managing and playing media **you have the right to use**. It does not supply, host or recommend any media.

- **You are responsible for what you add.** Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr, Prowlarr and the download clients act on the sources and titles you choose. Whether a source, tracker or release is lawful where you live is for you to determine.
- **Nothing broad is added for you.** A fresh install connects only a short starting set of sources that are lawful as they are (the Internet Archive, Linux distributions and live-music trading). Adding every public source is a deliberate click in Settings > Indexers, with a notice, and adult sources are never included in it.
- **YouTube downloads are opt-in.** The optional YouTube service (`--profile youtube`) can save videos from YouTube. Many concert uploads are unofficial and YouTube's terms restrict downloading, so it is off by default and only for what you have the right to keep.
- **Use it for your own household.** Do not run it as a service for the public, and do not share access to media you do not have the right to share.
- **Privacy tools are not permission.** A VPN or Tor changes who can see your traffic, not whether something is lawful. See [privacy](privacy.md).

If you find a default that leads people toward something they should not do, please report it as a bug.
